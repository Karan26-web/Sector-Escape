How to play

Watch the gap

Guardians pound on the barrier across the gap. A point at the centre draws the radius and sweeps out the sector.

Walk Karan to the glowing spot behind the centre (the stick, or W A S D).

Find the sector area

A = θ360 × π × r²

Every area is in terms of π: keep π in the answer.

The guardians freeze while you think, so take your time. Each miss shows you the next step of the working.

Fire!

You have 3 shields. Each wrong shot costs one.

Arenas 1–4: choose the right values. Arenas 5–7: type the number in front of π, like 12 for 12π.
