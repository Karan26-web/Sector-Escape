    How to play

    1. Enter the arena. The gate seals behind you. The arc on the far side is already open — and the guardians are standing in it.
    2. Run to the platform at the centre (WASD / arrows, or tap the floor). The Sector Laser Gun rises from under it.
    3. Read the battlefield. The gun scans the open arc: the radius r and central angle θ appear.
    4. Calculate the sector area with A = (θ/360) × πr², using π = 22/7.
    5. Enter the exact area and FIRE. Too small → some guardians survive and attack. Too large → the excess hits the solid wall and reflects back at you. Exact → every guardian destroyed; run through the open arc into the next ring.
