    How to play

    1. Enter the arena. The gate locks behind you and guardians pour through a breach in the wall.
    2. Run to the Sector Cannon at the centre (WASD / arrows, or tap the floor).
    3. Read the battlefield. The cannon scans the breach: the radius r and central angle θ appear.
    4. Calculate the sector area with A = (θ/360) × πr², using π = 22/7.
    5. Enter the exact area and FIRE. Too small → some guardians survive and attack. Too large → the excess energy reflects off the wall and hits you. Exact → every guardian destroyed and the exit unlocks.
